Initial Technical Assessment · Draft v1

AI Voice & Ordering Agent for a Pharmacy Franchise

Replacing the manual cashier / order-desk workflow with an AI agent across in-branch voice, WhatsApp and online channels, integrated natively with Odoo.

1Solution at a glance

A single AI agent receives customer and staff requests (voice in branch, WhatsApp, online, third-party leads), understands them in Arabic and English, checks stock, and creates and tracks the order directly in Odoo. It routes pickup or delivery, takes payment, and hands over to a human whenever the counter is busy or a request needs pharmacist judgment.

6Key risksAdded

Odoo 15 → 18 migrationLargest unknown. Custom modules may need rewriting, not just upgrading.
Pharmacy regulationControlled or prescription items need pharmacist sign-off. Rules depend on jurisdiction.
Arabic dialect accuracySpeech recognition in noisy branches needs real-world testing and tuning.
Customer data privacyHealth-related orders require clear data handling, retention and hosting decisions.
Hardware & latencySelf-hosted models need GPU capacity sized for peak voice load.

7Questions for discoveryAdded

  • How many branches, and what is daily order volume per branch?
